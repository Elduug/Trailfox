# Code of conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience levels. Focus criticism on ideas and code, accept feedback gracefully, and protect other people's private information.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, sexualized conduct, trolling, threats, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This standard applies in repository discussions, issues, pull requests, and other spaces where someone represents the project. The maintainer may edit, reject, or remove contributions and participation that do not meet it.

Report conduct concerns privately to [Jake Carter](https://github.com/Elduug) through GitHub. Reports will be reviewed fairly and with appropriate confidentiality.

This policy is informed by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
